The Firm

Established in 1997 by Robert Ongeri

Firm Background

Robert Case and Partners is a Nairobi based law firm with offices situated at Queensway House along the secure Standard Street next to Hilton Hotel in the heart of the central business district. We enjoy the convenience of close proximity to major financial, commercial and governmental institutions.

The firm was established by Robert Ongeri in 1997. Robert holds a Masters of Law Degree and has a rich legal expertise spanning over a period of 14 years. We are Certified Public Secretaries, Patent Agents and Legal Experts registered with the National Environmental Management Authority (NEMA).

Firm Attributes & Distinctiveness

The distinctiveness of our firm is that we have the ability to offer a variety of services under one roof and we are thereof apt in providing a more comprehensive and holistic resolution to our clients' legal problems.

The firm is strategically sized and configured to deliver the highest quality service to our clientele regardless of the complexity of the legal issues. Since its inception, the firm has dedicated itself to an excellent service by providing a full range of business and litigation legal services.

Vision and Mission Statement

At Robert Case and Partners, we consider the practice of law to be, first and foremost, about our clients. We are unwavering in our commitment to exceptional client service. We pride ourselves for the breadth and depth of our legal expertise and experience, and we apply that expertise and experience to help our clients succeed. We meet our clients' objectives by developing a strong grasp of their business and their industry and by building trusted relationships with their people.

We aim to develop and maintain a high level of performance, adhere to the ethical standards of our profession and endeavor to satisfy the needs of our clients and to promote the welfare of the staff and members of the firm.
