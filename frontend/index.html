Introduction & History

Robert Case and Partners Advocates (RCP) is a premier Nairobi-based law firm with strategic offices in Upper Hill at KMA Apartments and in the Nairobi Central Business District at Queensway House[cite: 1]. Originally founded in 2000 as ROA and Associates, the firm incorporated new partners over time to embrace a robust corporate and regional practice[cite: 1].

RCP operates as a 4-lawyer tier practice supported by experienced associates, trainee advocates, and technical personnel[cite: 1]. Our professionals hold specialized credentials as Certified Public Secretaries (CPS), Patent Agents with KIPI, Lead Experts registered with the National Environment Management Authority (NEMA), and Chartered Arbitrators (CIArb)[cite: 1].

Principal Attributes & Distinctiveness

Our core distinctiveness lies in offering a comprehensive suite of multidisciplinary legal services under one roof without burdening clients with added financial overhead[cite: 1]. We maintain a proactive approach aimed at concluding client matters with speed and high competence[cite: 1].

Our lawyers hold active memberships in prestigious national and international bodies, including the Law Society of Kenya (LSK), Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (CIArb - Kenya & UK), International Bar Association (IBA), International Trademark Association (INTA), and Commonwealth Lawyers Association (CLA)[cite: 1]. We maintain an affiliate partnership with CEC Consultants for environmental audits, subscribe to global legal publications like Martindale-Hubbell and IFLR, and possess extensive legal research resources[cite: 1].
