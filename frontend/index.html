Our Firm Overview

Robert Case & Partners is a Nairobi-based law firm situated at Queensway House along Kaunda Street in the Central Business District. We enjoy close proximity to major financial, commercial, and governmental institutions.

Established in 1997, our firm is strategically configured to deliver the highest quality legal services regardless of issue complexity, offering a full range of business and litigation expertise under one roof.
